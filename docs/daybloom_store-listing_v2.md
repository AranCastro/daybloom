## Full description (max 4,000 characters)

Daybloom is one calm app for your mood, your tasks, your focus and the people who lift you. Everything you do grows the same garden.

ONE TAP A DAY
Choose how today feels, from Bright to Heavy. Add a few feelings if you like, from twenty everyday words or your own word with your own emoji. Write small notes about the day, good or hard. Your streak allows a rest day, so one missed day does not undo a good week.

A GARDEN THAT GROWS WITH YOU
Checking in, finishing a task, a focus session, a short game or reaching out to someone each grows a flower. Collect twelve kinds, including the rare Neelakurinji, and a Golden Lotus every twentieth bloom. The garden follows the time of day and the season, and, if you switch on Live weather, the weather where you are.

PLAN WITH THE EISENHOWER MATRIX
Sort tasks into Do first, Schedule, Delegate and Later. Mark how much effort each takes, from Quick to Deep work; on a low-energy morning the matrix shows only what you can carry. Split big tasks into steps and get credit for each day you work on them.

ROUTINES THAT COME BACK
Medicines, a walk, watering the plants: set a task to repeat every day, on chosen weekdays or every few days, up to three times a day. It appears in its quadrant by itself, once, at the right time, with an optional quiet reminder you can tick from the notification.

FOCUS TIMER
Gentle 15, Classic 25 or Deep 50-minute sessions with breaks, and seven focus sounds plus tanpura and raga drones. The timer keeps running if you leave the app, and a flower opens when you finish.

YOUR CIRCLE AND A GENTLE NUDGE
Sort the people you trust by how close they are and how you like to reach them. On a low day Daybloom suggests one person to call and one to message. After a few low days in a row, one tap prepares a message to your chosen buddy on SMS or WhatsApp; you press Send.

CALM GAMES AND SMALL JOYS
Breathe, Bubble Pop, Pair Up and Colour Clash, with one picked for today's mood. A jar of good days that hands your own words back on a heavy day. Thottam, where you arrange your flowers into a Poo Kolam for Onam, Diwali or Pongal. Send a flower card to someone on WhatsApp.

HOME SCREEN WIDGETS
Eight widgets, light or dark, as transparent as you like: the full matrix, today's tasks with a lock and undo, one-tap check-in, the garden, your streak, a Pomodoro you can start and pause, and your circle.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no advertising, no tracking. Your moods, notes, tasks and garden stay on your phone and in backup files you choose to save. An optional app lock uses a PIN or your fingerprint. The only thing that leaves the phone is an approximate location, and only if you switch on Live weather, to fetch the weather from Open-Meteo.

Daybloom is a wellbeing and planning app, not a medical or emergency service. If you are in distress in India, call Tele-MANAS on 14416 (free, 24 hours) or emergency services on 112.
